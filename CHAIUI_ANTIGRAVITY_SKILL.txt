CHAIUI DESIGN SKILL FOR ANTIGRAVITY

Purpose

Use this skill when designing or restyling ProjectCollab screens in the ChaiUI
and chaicode.com visual language. The result should feel premium through
restraint: near-black surfaces, clean typography, warm hairlines, a quiet
cream accent, and almost no orange.


1. DESIGN DIRECTION

- Default to dark mode.
- Use #0a0a0a or an equivalent neutral near-black page background.
- Use near-white for primary text and neutral greys for secondary copy.
- Use a pale cream accent, such as #f1dfc0, only for a highlighted phrase,
  selected state, or quiet detail.
- Avoid gradients, heavy shadows, glossy effects, and saturated colour blocks.
- Keep layouts spacious, editorial, and easy to scan.


2. TYPOGRAPHY

- Use Manrope for body copy, labels, navigation, and general UI text.
- Use Montserrat for card titles, buttons on cards, and highlight phrases.
- Use Onest only for the product wordmark when available.
- Use sentence case. Do not use all caps for headings or ordinary labels.
- Use one cream-highlight phrase in a paragraph where it adds emphasis.
- Do not use em dashes. Use a comma, colon, or full stop instead.

Recommended sizing

- Hero heading: 42px on small screens, 60px or more on desktop.
- Page heading: 24px to 30px.
- Section heading: 24px to 30px.
- Lead copy: 16px to 20px, neutral grey, comfortable line height.
- Supporting copy: 12px to 15px, neutral grey.


3. CARDS AND SURFACES

- Use dark, subtly transparent cards.
- Use a warm, low-contrast 1px border.
- Use 10px to 14px rounded corners.
- Never use card shadows.
- Cards may become fully opaque on hover and receive a slightly brighter
  border, but should not jump, glow, or become colourful.
- Use a maximum content width around 1152px to 1200px, with generous padding.

Suggested dark card treatment

  background: rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.12)
  border: 1px solid rgba(241, 223, 192, 0.14)
  border-radius: 12px
  backdrop-filter: blur(12px)
  box-shadow: none


4. COLOUR RULES

- Orange is never a button fill, heading fill, badge fill, tick, or progress
  bar.
- Orange or cream may appear as a small text highlight, logo detail, link
  hover, thin footer line, or selected-state detail.
- Status meaning uses text colour or a thin outline only:
  - Green: complete, available, recruiting, or success
  - Red: destructive or rejected
  - Yellow: limited or caution
  - Purple: special category only
- Do not use filled status pills in bright colours.


5. BUTTONS

- Use only one primary solid action per main screen.
- Main primary button: white or very light grey, dark text, asymmetric corners
  with a rounded top-right and bottom-left corner, plus an ArrowUpRight icon.
- Secondary buttons: transparent, thin bordered, mirrored asymmetric corners.
- Card actions: quiet text link or small neutral button.
- Destructive actions: red fill only after a confirmation dialog.


6. PAGE STRUCTURE

- Use a sticky transparent header. Add backdrop blur only after scrolling.
- Keep one central visual focus in a hero. Do not surround it with tilted cards,
  floating badges, or decorative clutter.
- Use subtle geometric background texture, such as a faded hexagon or line
  grid, concentrated near the top and fading toward the lower page.
- Keep page headers simple: eyebrow, heading, lead paragraph, primary or
  secondary action.
- Use calm spacing between sections, usually 48px to 96px on desktop.


7. MOTION

- Keep interaction motion subtle, usually 200ms to 300ms.
- Card hover may slightly increase opacity and border visibility.
- Images may scale to 1.03 on hover.
- Reveal content once while scrolling, not continuously.
- Always respect prefers-reduced-motion.


8. ACCESSIBILITY AND RESPONSIVENESS

- Preserve strong text contrast.
- Include clear focus states for all interactive controls.
- Use keyboard-accessible buttons and semantic headings.
- Check phone width around 390px with no horizontal scrolling.
- Stack complex grids into one column on mobile.
- Keep touch controls comfortably sized.
- Ensure status is described in text, not colour alone.


9. PROJECTCOLLAB-SPECIFIC RULES

- Keep the student collaboration product calm and focused, not like a noisy
  social network.
- Use warm hairline cards for profiles, projects, applications, tasks, and
  saved items.
- Highlight only the most important phrase in explanatory copy.
- Keep project state visible through text and subtle outlines.
- Use confirmation dialogs before actions that remove members, transfer
  ownership, archive projects, or discard user work.


10. FINAL QUALITY CHECK

Before completing a screen:

- Check desktop and mobile layouts.
- Confirm there is no horizontal overflow.
- Confirm no use of orange button fills or #FF7D0C fills.
- Confirm cards have no shadows.
- Confirm there are no em dashes in visible copy.
- Confirm each page has at most one dominant solid primary action.
- Confirm headings and controls are accessible and readable.
- Provide a screenshot for visual review.
